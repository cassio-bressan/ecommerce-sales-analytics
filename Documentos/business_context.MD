# Business Context

## Overview

TheLook is a fictional e-commerce company specializing in clothing and fashion products. The company generates operational data from customers, orders, products and sales transactions through its online platform.

As the business has grown, the volume of available data has increased significantly, making it difficult for the commercial team to monitor performance using operational tables alone.

This project simulates a real business scenario in which the Data Analytics team is responsible for designing an analytical solution that supports commercial decision-making.

---

## Business Problem

The Commercial Manager currently lacks a centralized analytical view of the company's sales performance.

Although transactional data is available, there is no structured environment to answer questions such as:

- How are sales evolving over time?
- Which products and categories generate the highest revenue?
- Who are the most valuable customers?
- What are the main sales trends and performance indicators?

As a result, business decisions are based on fragmented information and manual analysis.

---

## Business Objective

Develop an analytical solution capable of transforming operational data into meaningful business information.

The solution should provide reliable metrics and visual dashboards that support strategic and operational decisions made by the Commercial Manager.

---

## Scope

The first version of the project focuses exclusively on the commercial domain.

The following tables are included:

| Table | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| users | Customer information |
| orders | Orders placed by customers |
| order_items | Products sold in each order |
| products | Product catalog |

The following tables are intentionally excluded from the project:

| Table | Reason |
|--------|--------|
| events | Website navigation events are outside the commercial scope |
| inventory_items | Inventory management is not part of the business problem |
| distribution_centers | Logistics data is outside the objectives of the project |
